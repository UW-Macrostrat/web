Macrostrat maintains a strong integration with [xDD](https://xdd.wisc.edu/), a platform
for discovering data from the scientific literature. xDD supplies the paper text and
citations that Macrostrat's [knowledge graph](/knowledge-graph) is built from.

## Capabilities

- Stratigraphic units linked to papers in Rockd and Macrostrat
- Source text for the [knowledge graph](/knowledge-graph) — entities extracted from papers, reviewed and corrected at [/knowledge-graph/source-texts](/knowledge-graph/source-texts)
- Training machine learning models for [Map legend affinity](/dev/map/legend-affinity)
