Macrostrat maintains a strong integration with [xDD](https://xdd.wisc.edu/), a platform
for discovering data from the scientific literature. Machine-learning models extract
geologic entities (stratigraphic names, lithologies, intervals, …) from paper text
into Macrostrat's knowledge graph, and reviewers correct those extractions here.

## Capabilities

- Stratigraphic units linked to papers in Rockd and Macrostrat
- [Extractions](/integrations/xdd/extractions) — papers with structured data extracted from their text
- [Feedback](/integrations/xdd/feedback) — review and correct extractions, one source text at a time
- [Model runs](/integrations/xdd/runs) and [entity types](/integrations/xdd/types)
- Training machine learning models for [Map legend affinity](/dev/map/legend-affinity)
