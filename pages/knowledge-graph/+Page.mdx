Macrostrat's **knowledge graph** links geologic entities in the scientific
literature — stratigraphic names, lithologies, intervals, minerals, and their
relationships — to Macrostrat's own data. Machine-learning models extract
entities from paper text supplied by [xDD](/integrations/xdd); reviewers
correct those extractions here, and the corrected graph feeds back into
Macrostrat.

## Browse and review

- [Papers](/knowledge-graph/papers) — publications with extracted entities, and each paper's source texts
- [Source texts](/knowledge-graph/source-texts) — the review queue: read, correct, and save one paragraph at a time
