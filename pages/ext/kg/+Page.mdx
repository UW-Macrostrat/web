Macrostrat's **knowledge graph** links geologic entities in the scientific
literature — stratigraphic names, lithologies, intervals, minerals, and their
relationships — to Macrostrat's own data. Models extract entities from paper
text supplied by [xDD](/integrations/xdd); reviewers correct them here.

This is an in-progress research project, kept apart from Macrostrat's core data.

- [Papers](/ext/kg/papers) — publications with extractions, and each paper's source texts
- [Source texts](/ext/kg/source-texts) — the review queue: correct one paragraph at a time
